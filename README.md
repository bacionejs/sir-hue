Sir Hue the unicorn works for the Royal Academy of Rainbow Standards and is tasked with retrieving balloons that **collect Rainbow Data**.

**He must collect them in the correct rainbow order**
- Starts at 0 degrees (red) and wraps at 360. For reference, look at a 360-degree HSL color wheel.
- Each **advance must be < 90 degrees**, forcing you to think in finer detail than just red, green, and blue. A safe first balloon is **orange**.
- If you make a **mistake, the score is decremented and sets you back to red**.
